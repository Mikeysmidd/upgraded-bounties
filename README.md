# The bounties
Every Boss Blind in Balatro pays its own bounty. Beat the boss, and Cash Out opens a small pack holding that boss's reward.

## What This does

Each boss will always pay the same bounty, so the boss that you face will always give the same reward. Bounties also stack: if you beat the same boss twice, you get its reward, twice.

## How it works

- **The bounty pack opens at Cash Out after Boss Blind**, before the shop. The Cash Out button turns red and says "+ Bounty" when one is waiting. Take the card to apply it, or Skip to give it up.
- **Choosing screens.** The Hook, Cerulean Bell, The Flint and The Serpent all open a screen when you press the "use" button, pressing Back closes the window without using it.
-**Amber Acorn and Crimson Heart** act on the Joker you have highlighted: click a Joker, then press Use. Amber Acorn needs a free Joker slot, and its copy is never Negative.
- **The Serpent's 3 cards** get a random rank and suit when you beat it. The screen shows each card before you pick its Enhancement.
- **Violet Vessel's Soul** can be used with a full Joker row. A copy of it (for example from Perkeo) is a normal Negative The Soul.
- **Deck-wide bounties** (the suits, The Mark, The Plant) apply to the cards you own when you take them. Suits go by the card's printed suit and faces by rank, so Wild Cards, Smeared Joker and Pareidolia don't stretch them. Stone Cards are skipped.
- **The Arm's levels** skip any hand the next boss debuffs.
- **Disabled bosses still pay.** If Chicot or Luchador disabled the boss, The Mouth levels the last hand you played, The Tooth pays double what it took ($0 if nothing), and The Pillar still pays for the cards you played.


### Config
- **enable boss bounties** (on by default).
- **Test mode** (Usually off by default).
